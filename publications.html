IEEE Conference Paper 2025

TamilNews Inspector: A Multilingual Fake News Detection Framework Using Sentence Embeddings and Ensemble Learning

S. Doss M, M. T. Deepak, K. S. Hari Kowshik, A. KB, S. S, P. S

2025 Innovations in Power and Advanced Computing Technologies (i-PACT) · Surabaya, Indonesia · pp. 1-6

A multilingual fake news detection framework targeting Tamil and English news, combining transformer-based semantic embeddings (distiluse-base-multilingual-cased-v1) with an XGBoost ensemble classifier. The system classifies news into six misinformation categories — Fake, Real, Satire, Clickbait, Partially True, and Unverified — trained on 10,000 Tamil & English samples from 2023 to March 2025.

Deployed as a Gradio web interface with real-time and batch inference, the framework achieves 97.3% classification accuracy, outperforming traditional baselines (Logistic Regression, SVM, Random Forest, kNN) while remaining more computationally efficient than BERT-based approaches. Designed for low-resource multilingual settings with planned extensions to Explainable AI and other Indian languages.
